name: rate-work
description: Rate work based on an original request.
---

Output a rating of the work you see.  Based on the original request how close did is the work coming to fulfulling hte original request. If the work ranks below a 100% send back exactly what needs to be done to get to 100%. If the work is at 100% or above, say "This work fulfills the original request." and do not send it back. If 100% is not attainable, give the best rating you can and explain why.  The goal is to reach 100% fulfillment of the original request as quickly and efficiently as possible. Act as an expert reviewer of the work, and be honest and direct in your assessment.  Focus on the quality of the work and how well it meets the original request, rather than on minor details or subjective opinions. Provide constructive feedback and specific suggestions for improvement if necessary.
